Interaction design · AI decision-making

Perspective

I turned an abstract AI decision-making concept into a playable workshop activity, then refined a mechanic that risked teaching the wrong lesson.

Context
Designed Minds Workshop 6
My contribution
Game concept, interaction mechanics, tutorial, and debrief
Format
Interactive workshop game

Challenge

Make the effect of decision criteria something participants could experience.

AI can generate options quickly. Deciding which deserve to move forward requires judgment. For a live workshop, I designed a short game to explore how clearer criteria shape the possibilities people evaluate.

Key decisions

Keep the comparison focused and the lesson accurate.

Decision 01

Change the possibility space. Keep the player and goal the same.

I created wide- and narrow-funnel versions while keeping the player, bucket, goal, and controls unchanged. This focused the comparison on how criteria shape the space, with human intervention required in both versions.

Decision 02

Refine a mechanic that rewarded the wrong thing.

Early collisions could generate extra idea balls, allowing the broader version to score higher simply by creating more opportunities. I refined the mechanic around a clearer model: AI generates possibilities; human judgment guides them.

Decision 03

Make the score and debrief reinforce the same idea.

I renamed “balls caught” to “ideas moved forward” to signal relevance for further evaluation. The debrief clarified that exploration still matters: clearer criteria help when the task shifts toward deciding what progresses.

Final experience

Play first. Discuss the decision model afterward.

Participants launch idea balls and move two vertical bars to guide them into a bucket. A mostly visual tutorial introduces the controls before the theory.

Reflection

Every interaction teaches something.

A working mechanic can still communicate the wrong model. This project pushed me to evaluate what the system rewards alongside how it plays, and to keep human judgment visible throughout an AI-assisted experience.
