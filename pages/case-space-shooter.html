AI-assisted building · First experiment

Space Shooter

I used ChatGPT to turn a rough game idea into a build plan, then worked through it in Cursor to create my first playable AI-assisted experiment.

Context
My first experiment building with AI
My contribution
Concept exploration, build direction, and output review
Tools
ChatGPT and Cursor

Challenge

Could AI help me take a rough game idea all the way to something playable?

I started with a question rather than a detailed specification. I wanted to explore a simple game, find a direction small enough to build, and understand how AI could help me get there.

Early conversations with ChatGPT explored familiar mechanics, including Flappy Bird-style interaction. Comparing those possibilities gradually led me toward a space rocket game: Space Shooter.

Key decisions

Explore the idea, then give the build a clear structure.

Decision 01

Use conversation to find a manageable direction.

I used ChatGPT to brainstorm concepts, compare simple mechanics, and narrow the idea. This helped me turn an open-ended interest in making a game into a small, buildable interaction.

Decision 02

Break implementation into sequential prompts.

Once the concept was clear, I asked ChatGPT to create step-by-step implementation prompts. I took those prompts into Cursor one at a time, giving me a path through the build and opportunities to review the result as it developed.

Decision 03

Give each tool a role while keeping the direction with me.

ChatGPT helped me think through the concept and structure the work. Cursor helped translate those instructions into a working interactive experience. I decided what to make, evaluated the output, and guided the process.

Final experience

A playable game, and a workflow I could use again.

The experiment resulted in a working space shooter game. Moving from a conversation to sequential implementation prompts helped me reach a playable result faster than figuring out the entire implementation path myself.

The workflow was simple: ideate in ChatGPT, define the game, generate build prompts, implement in Cursor, and review the result.

Reflection

I didn’t need a perfect specification to start building.

I learned that I could explore, narrow, structure, and build with AI supporting each step differently. The value extended beyond generating code: AI helped me move from an unclear idea to a structured, buildable interaction.

This became the starting point for how I approached more complex AI-assisted prototypes and design-to-code work.
