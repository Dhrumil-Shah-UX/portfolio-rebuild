AI for Designers

A grounding session for designers to understand what AI is, what it can and cannot do, and how to approach AI tools more intentionally instead of relying on trial and error.

My Role
Co-designed and co-hosted
Audience
Designers, product teams, and curious builders
Format
90-minute interactive virtual workshop · 68 joined

Key takeaways

  1. Understand AI fundamentals and where its limits are.
  2. Build an intentional workflow instead of copying prompts.
  3. Explore how AI can support everyday design work.
