Space Shooter

From a rough idea to a playable first build.

I used ChatGPT to explore a game concept and create sequential build prompts, then worked through them in Cursor while directing and reviewing the output.

Context
My first experiment building with AI
My contribution
Concept exploration, build direction, and output review
Tools
ChatGPT and Cursor
AI involvement
ChatGPT supported ideation and build prompts; Cursor supported implementation. I chose the direction and evaluated the result.

Problem

Could a vague idea become something I could actually play?

I wanted a small enough experiment to move through the complete build process. Early conversations explored familiar mechanics, including Flappy Bird-style interaction, before narrowing toward a space rocket game.

Decisions

Explore, structure, build—and review.

01/03Narrow the idea

Choose a manageable direction before implementation.

I used ChatGPT to brainstorm and compare simple mechanics. The goal was a small, buildable interaction, not a broad game specification with an expanding feature list.

Evidence boundary: the recording demonstrates the game interface. It is not a user test, performance benchmark, or evidence of an autonomous in-game AI system.

02/03Structure the build

Work through implementation prompts in sequence.

Once the concept was clear, I asked ChatGPT for step-by-step implementation prompts and took them into Cursor one at a time. This gave me a way to review the developing result rather than ask for the entire game in one step.

03/03Retain direction

Give each tool a role without giving away the decisions.

ChatGPT helped with thinking and structure; Cursor helped turn instructions into code. I decided what to build, evaluated the output, and guided the next step. The experiment became a starting point for later AI-assisted prototypes.
