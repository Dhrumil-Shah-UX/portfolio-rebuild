Problem

A building address needed a clear path to action.

The initial goal was to help people discover NYC buildings and access useful property information. The design challenge was keeping that building context intact as they moved toward an inquiry.

A changing brief
A new stakeholder direction required substantial redesign and redevelopment.
Design meets implementation
Search, forms and a custom WebGL interaction had to fit the website experience.

Decisions

Connect the entry point, the inquiry and the implementation.

The work evolved with the brief. Four decisions show how I moved between UX structure, stakeholder requirements and working code.

01/04Adapt the website direction

Rebuild the entry point around the revised brief.

I revisited the landing page and inquiry flow when the stakeholder introduced a different visual and structural direction. The archive shows the move from a brand-led announcement to a prominent building-address entry.

My responsibility in the change I redesigned and redeveloped the Wix landing page and building inquiry. The revised visual and structural direction came from the stakeholder; it differed from my initial UX recommendation.

02/04Keep property context

Make the building part of the inquiry.

I brought NYC address search into the inquiry form. The recorded design connects a property-detail area, an Add action and an added-properties list so the inquiry can retain the selected building.

Identify the property
The recorded detail fields include name, address, BIN, BBL, borough, use type and floor area.
Keep the next step explicit
Add the property to the inquiry; a separate address field supports other cities.

The archive documents the interface; end-to-end submission and property-data accuracy remain unverified.

03/04Carry context across pages

Carry the selected building into the detailed result.

In my search-related Next.js work, I added public search and preview endpoints and building deep links. A selected building ID connects the compact preview to its detailed result, with explicit loading and not-found states.

Keep responses focused

The public contract defines compact search and preview fields rather than exposing raw database rows.

Handle incomplete paths

Source tests cover short queries, no results, invalid IDs, unavailable previews and origin checks. Production acceptance results are unavailable.

Implementation evidence My July 2026 commits add the public API, deep-link support and loading feedback. The reviewed search accepts at least three characters and returns up to eight results.

04/04Build a focused brand interaction

Let the particles move while the words stay recognizable.

I developed the “AI - Enhanced MEP” particle treatment and embedded the separately hosted Three.js demo in a Wix HTML component. Pointer motion influences the particles; shape constraints keep the text recognizable.

Bind motion to the text

Canvas text is sampled into point positions. A text mask, spring forces and a leash constrain the moving particles around the letter shapes.

Bound the workload

The source caps particle speed and device pixel ratio. These controls limit the workload; no FPS improvement was measured.
